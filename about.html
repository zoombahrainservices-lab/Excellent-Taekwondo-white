ABOUT US

Building Champions Since 2015

OUR STORY

WHO WE ARE

Excellent Taekwondo is Bahrain's premier martial arts and fitness academy, dedicated to empowering individuals through the transformative power of martial arts and athletic training.

Founded in 2015 in the heart of Adliya, Manama, we have grown from a small Taekwondo dojang to a comprehensive training facility offering multiple disciplines. Our journey began with a simple vision: to create a space where people of all ages and abilities could discover their potential through martial arts.

Today, we serve over 500 active students, from young children taking their first steps in martial arts to adults pursuing fitness goals and competitive athletes training for championships. Our success is built on the foundation of excellent instruction, modern facilities, and a supportive community that celebrates every achievement.

OUR PURPOSE

MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To provide world-class martial arts and fitness training that transforms lives through discipline, physical excellence, and mental strength. We are committed to:

  • Delivering exceptional instruction from certified professionals
  • Creating a safe, inclusive, and supportive training environment
  • Developing character, confidence, and leadership skills
  • Fostering a community united by the pursuit of excellence
  • Promoting health, fitness, and well-being for all ages

Our Vision

To be recognized as the leading martial arts and fitness academy in Bahrain and the GCC region, known for:

  • Excellence in martial arts instruction and competition success
  • Innovation in training methods and programs
  • Development of national and international champions
  • Contribution to the growth of martial arts in Bahrain
  • Positive impact on our community and students' lives
OUR APPROACH

TRAINING PHILOSOPHY

Our teaching philosophy is rooted in traditional martial arts values combined with modern training science and sports psychology.

Student-Centered

We recognize that every student is unique. Our programs are tailored to individual goals, abilities, and learning styles, ensuring personalized attention and progress.

Excellence Focus

We set high standards and provide the tools, training, and support needed to achieve them. Excellence is not just a goal—it's our standard in everything we do.

Community Built

We foster a supportive community where students encourage each other, celebrate achievements together, and grow as a team while pursuing individual goals.

Holistic Development

Training extends beyond physical skills. We develop mental strength, emotional resilience, and life skills that benefit students in all areas of life.

Safety First

Safety is paramount. Our facilities, equipment, and training methods prioritize injury prevention while maximizing learning and performance.

Continuous Growth

We believe in lifelong learning. Our instructors continuously update their skills, and we encourage students to embrace a growth mindset in their training journey.
