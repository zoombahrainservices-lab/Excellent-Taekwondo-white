## Image Guidelines

### Quality
- Use high-resolution images
- Ensure good lighting and clarity
- Show action, movement, and energy
- Authentic photos of actual training sessions preferred

### Format
- **Primary format**: JPEG (.jpg)
- **For graphics with transparency**: PNG (.png)
- **Modern alternative**: WebP (.webp) for better compression

### Optimization
Before uploading, optimize images using:
- **TinyPNG** (tinypng.com)
- **Squoosh** (squoosh.app)
- **ImageOptim** (imageoptim.com)

Target file sizes:
- Hero backgrounds: < 500KB
- Class images: < 200KB
- Instructor photos: < 150KB
- Gallery images: < 200KB

### Content Suggestions

#### Hero Background
- Dynamic Taekwondo kick or action pose
- Black background or dark training environment
- Motion blur to convey energy
- Red accents if possible

#### Class Images
- Show actual classes in action
- Include instructors and students
- Proper lighting and professional quality
- Diverse age groups when applicable

#### Instructor Photos
- Professional headshots or action shots
- Clear face visibility
- Wearing instructor uniform/attire
- Neutral or training background

#### Gallery Images
- Variety of activities and age groups
- Training moments, celebrations, competitions
- Show facility and equipment
- Capture emotion and achievement

## Naming Convention

✅ **Good**: `taekwondo-class.jpg`, `instructor-1.jpg`
❌ **Avoid**: `IMG_1234.jpg`, `photo.jpg`, `image (1).jpg`

- Use lowercase letters
- Use hyphens (-) not underscores (_) or spaces
- Be descriptive and specific
- Keep names short but meaningful

## Copyright & Attribution

- Use only images you have rights to use
- If using stock photos, ensure they're free for commercial use
- Credit photographers if required by license
- Get permission from people in photos (model releases)
