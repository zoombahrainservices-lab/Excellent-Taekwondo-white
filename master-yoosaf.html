Master Muhammed Yoosaf MA

Taekwondo Instructor

Muhammed Yoosaf MA is a dedicated Taekwondo instructor with a 2nd Dan Black Belt certification. He is committed to teaching the fundamental principles of martial arts and helping students develop both physical skills and mental discipline.

Master Yoosaf brings passion and dedication to every class, focusing on proper technique, character development, and instilling the values of respect and perseverance in his students. His patient teaching style makes him especially effective with younger students and beginners.
